Juridik & support

English

Legal & support

hittamin GPS is an iOS app that shows your GPS position in several formats (degrees, DMS, UTM, MGRS), an Apple map with a route track, a full-screen speedometer, and export via copy, TXT, PDF or the iOS share sheet. The app has no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking – all data stays on your device until you choose to share it.
